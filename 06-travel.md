# Root & Claw — Travel

## Travel Overview

In many TO-based games action happens in dangerous worlds where protected refuges are rare and separated by significant distances. To survive, must travel - so traveling on foot or with transport can become one of main games.

## Travel Map

### Hexagonal Sectors

- Usually map divided into hexagonal sectors allowing distance measurement and influencing hero movement
- Distance between opposite corners of sector = 10 kilometers
- Some games use square sectors instead

## Tasks

At start of each shift, every traveler must choose one task dedicating most of their time. Simultaneously doing two activities (including travel) not allowed, except observing during travel. Most tasks can be shared by several characters, but some require choosing one executor.

### Task List

- **Hiking**: Walking. One character can combine with observation.
- **Vehicle Operation**: Managing land, water, or flying transport. Passengers can observe, rest or sleep during movement.
- **Supply Gathering**: Collecting wood or grain for producing alcoholic fuel. Must hike.
- **Observation**: Surveying surroundings for enemies and random encounters. Only one character can do this. Can combine with hiking.
- **Types of Terrain**: Various terrain types affect movement speed and checks for finding supplies, searching, and hunting (see below)
- **Roads**: Usually several main routes on map. Traveling along them is faster but also increases chance of random encounters. Only main routes count as roads even if countryside full of trails and small dirt tracks.
- **River**: Not terrain type per se, but can travel along. To cross river - need ford or bridge. If none - can cross by boat, raft or swimming.
- **Food Gathering**: Searching for edible plants and herbs. Must hike.
- **Hunting**: Tracking and killing animals for food. Must hike.
- **Fishing**: Necessary near lake or river. Also possible from moving boat.
- **Camp Setup**: Only one character sets up camp. Others can help.
- **Food Preparation**: Requires kitchen or campfire (can kindle during camp setup).
- **Rest**: Can rest in moving transport if not driving and not observing.
- **Sleep**: Must sleep at least one shift per day (see "Sleep Deprivation" Chapter 4). Can sleep in moving transport if not driving and not observing.
- **Investigation**: When heroes stop in some place, journey interrupted. Can take from shift to several days or weeks. During investigation characters may need rest or sleep, but not counted if more than half designated shift spent investigating.

## Movement

### Hiking

- On road and open terrain under favorable conditions: two sectors per shift.
- Any other terrain without road: one sector per shift.
- During heavy rain travel: make Endurance check. On failure hero can move one sector less (may mean not crossing any sector).
- If only part of group succeeds - decide: wait for stragglers or leave them behind.
- If heroes in a hurry: can attempt to extend journey to three shifts per day. Each member must make Endurance check. Failure means character cannot continue hiking this shift: must rest or sleep. If only part succeeds - same decision as above.
- If very urgent: can attempt fourth shift per day. Each member must make Endurance check with -2 modifier. On failure same as above.

### March-break

- Without problems hero can hike for two shifts per day. If necessary can increase to three shifts. Each member must make Endurance check. Failure means character cannot continue this shift: must rest or sleep.
- If only part succeeds - heroes can split or stop together for rest.
- If heroes very hurried: can attempt fourth shift per day. Each member must make Endurance check with -2 modifier. On failure same as above.

### Navigation

When group moving on foot or transport wants to transition from sector without road to next, one character must make Survival check. On failure - heroes end up not in desired sector but in adjacent one. Roll any die: even = right sector, odd = left sector.

### Transport Movement

In near-modern game world all characters likely know how to operate civilian transport in normal conditions. From Chapter 4 vehicle list maximum movement in 10-kilometer sectors per shift. First value indicates speed on road, second off-road. If second value is dash - transport cannot move outside roads. Usually whole shift travel either on road or off-road, but gamemaster may allow splitting time equally at discretion.

## Random Encounters

In speed of movement included short stops. However delay more than several minutes (e.g., due to random encounter or other circumstances) won't allow heroes cross entire distance this shift. Last word on how many sectors heroes still managed to cross remains with gamemaster.

### While Hiking

- Every shift there's chance of random encounter
- Distance to encounter depends on terrain type (see table above)
- When gamemaster says random encounter impending, scout makes passive Insight check (special effort not allowed)
- On success - scout notices encounter group before they notice heroes
- Heroes can decide: hide, retreat or set ambush
- On failure - other group notices heroes first

### While Traveling by Transport

- If heroes travel by transport and encounter group on foot - scout gets -2 modifier to Insight check
- If heroes hike and encounter group by transport - scout gets +2 modifier
- If both groups by transport - no modifiers

### Hiding

If heroes not noticed, they can retreat and bypass random encounter by moving one sector through off-road terrain (with terrain modifiers). Last word on exact sector where this happens remains with gamemaster.

## Fuel

- To move, transport needs fuel. Amount in liters per sector depends on specific game world.
- When moving off-road fuel consumption doubles.

## Animals

- Can travel only two shifts per day.
- To travel additional shift - must make Dexterity check. On failure mount starts limping and falls powerless.
- Mount animals need at least one shift rest per day.
- While combat on foot or vehicle: see Chapter 4 combat rules.

## Night Travel

- Moving through off-road at night reduces speed by half (round up).
- Night travel through wilderness requires Endurance check; result affects next day's travel.

## Rest

- At camp end of day, time to set up camp: takes full shift (usually evening): find suitable spot, kindle campfire, prepare sleep places. Not necessary to kindle fire if desired (but smoke reveals camp location to everyone in area).
- Characters trying to sleep without fire or other heat source must make Endurance check to not freeze (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4), unless weather quite warm.
- Sleeping without campfire or other heat source: each hero must make Endurance check to not freeze.

## Sleep

- Heroes must sleep at least one shift per day (usually night).
- However if significant event happens this shift (e.g., battle or similar) sleep not counted.
- Can sleep in moving transport if not driving and not observing.

## Investigation

When heroes stop in some place, journey interrupted. Investigation can take from shift to several days or weeks. During investigation characters may need rest or sleep, but not counted if more than half designated shift spent investigating.

## Travel by Water

Having boat or raft allows crossing rivers, not using bridges or fords. Also can travel along rivers, lakes or oceans. These travels work same as land transport. If passengers not engaged in observation, fishing or similar tasks, can rest and even sleep.

### River Travel

- Can travel along rivers, lakes or oceans.
- If passengers not engaged in observation, fishing or similar tasks, can rest and even sleep.

## On Wild Land

In wilderness can sleep under standing tree without setting up camp. Saves time, but each hero must make Survival check to find suitable spot. Failure means character didn't rest. Since no fire kindled, heroes also suffer from cold (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4).

## On Snowy Ground

On snow can sleep without setting up camp. Saves time, but each hero must make Endurance check to not freeze (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4).

## Cooking Food

Processing collected vegetables, hunted meat and caught fish requires access to kitchen or at least pot. In last case per shift hero can prepare up to dozen daily rations. Includes butchering, gutting and cleaning fish etc. One character can cook while another sets up camp. Requires Survival check. Regardless result, raw vegetables, meat or fish transform into ready-to-eat rations at ratio 1 to 1. However on failure each who eats must make resistance check against food poisoning (for plants toxicity 3/D, for meat and fish - 6/D). Gamemaster can make check secretly.

At campfire excellent opportunity to improve health (see "Recovery" Chapter 4). However if significant event this shift (e.g., battle or similar) rest not counted.
