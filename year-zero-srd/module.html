    Lore

    Root & Claw · Module one

    Mutations and skills

    The world after the Tree is full of animals who stand upright, talk, and carry a little too much of everything inside them. This module adds what makes them heroes: a lineage, a dozen skills with new names, two kinds of mutation, an aura, and the traits of a beast.

    Bio-mutations
    The body rewrites itself and keeps the change: claws, hide, gills, spores. They never cost aura and they never turn off.
    Psi-mutations
    The mind pushes outward: spark, blaze, telekinesis, healing. Each use is a slow action and costs nothing; a curse on its die adds 1 psychosis.
    01

    The world after the Tree

    The Tree came after the people. Its roots went through the roads and the towers and drank what was underneath, and in the centuries since, the animals that survived it grew into the space people left: upright, handed, and talkative. Some are furred, some scaled, some feathered. All of them are a little wrong, in the way that a thing built from whatever was lying around is wrong.

    Tribes keep the old machinery running where they can and worship it where they cannot. Between the tribe fires lie the ruins, the mushroom forests and the rot — a sickness that turns a living body into a garden. Everyone who walks out of a village is either running from the rot or looking for something that might stop it.

    Heroes in this world are mutants, and the mutation is not a monster's curse. It is the thing that let them survive: the extra heart, the second stomach, the arm that ends in a blade of bone. A mutant is measured by what the body has become and by what the mind can push out of it.

    What this module changes

    Checks, damage, initiative, healing and travel work exactly as the core books describe. The module adds lineages, mutations, aura and beast traits, and it renames five of the twelve skills. Nothing else in the system moves.

    02

    Lineages

    A lineage is what the hero's ancestors were before the Tree. It gives one step in a skill at creation and one beast trait, free. A lineage is not a species lock: a raccoon-kin can be a brawler and a hare-kin can be a sniper, they just start from a different corner. The nine below are the common ones around the tribes; a village on the edge of the map may be something else entirely, and the master can build it from the same parts.

    Example

    Vess is raccoon-kin: Crafting at 2 instead of 1, and Keen paws. At creation she takes Echo sense as her second trait, because she is the one who goes into the dark tunnels first.

    Rolling is unchanged: the skill step plus the attribute, in whichever variant the table plays. Specializations exist as in the core books and cost 10 XP. A character with a beast-related specialty — tracking by scent, riding a war-boar, handling a hive — gets the usual modifier from it.

    Animal Ken is not a leash

    A beast does not obey a good roll; it does what a beast does. Animal Ken buys understanding and a moment of cooperation — it does not turn a wild thing into a servant, and it never works twice on the same frightened animal in one scene.

    04

    05

    Psi-mutations and aura

    A psi-mutation is the mind reaching past the body. Using one is a slow action and costs nothing; a curse on its die adds 1 psychosis, whether it works or not. Roll Wits plus the power's grade: grade I, II and III are +1, +2 and +3 dice in the classic variant, and a d6, d8 and d10 in the modern one. One success is enough; extra successes buy damage, targets or duration, agreed with the master.

    Psi-mutations
    PowerRangeEffect
    SparkShortDamage 1 plus the grade. A metal-clad target loses its next fast action.
    BlazeShortDamage 2 and everything flammable in the zone catches fire.
    Mucus bubbleShortOpposed by Acrobatics: the target is stuck and needs a slow action and a Force roll to get free. On yourself, it slows a fall.
    TelekinesisMediumLift and move an object up to your own weight. Grade III can hurl it for 2 damage.
    FungiShortA spore cloud fills the zone: everyone inside takes −2 on all rolls for one round.
    MendEngagedHeal 1 damage per success, on yourself or another. It does not touch stress or critical injuries.
    Beast callLongCall one animal of the area; it follows simple commands for a scene, then leaves.
    BlinkShortStep into a zone you can see, ignoring whatever stands between.

    Psychosis

    Psychosis is a track from 0 to 10 and the only price of magic. Keep it in the open: the player should see that two more curses turn the hero into a beast.

    Psychosis: the 0-10 track
    PsychosisStateWhat it means at the table
    0-2ClearMagic has left no trace yet: the hero sleeps, eats and talks like a person.
    3-4Beast under the skinOnly your own people see the signs: pupils, smell, growling in sleep. No rule changes yet.
    5-6On the edgeThe hero catches himself in beastly urges instead of words; to strangers he is already strange.
    7-8Breakdown closeHeld together by habit and by his own. Being left alone is a bad idea.
    9One step from the beastAny new curse or shock takes him to 10.
    10InstinctAn Empathy save at the start of each round; a failure shrinks the pool by one die. Pool gone means full transformation.

    What raises psychosis: a curse on a spell die, +1 at rank 1, +2 at rank 2, +3 at rank 3 and above; a curse on a psi-power, +1; shocks, +1 to +3 (a friend's death, betrayal, a broken oath, the loss of a body part). What lowers it: −1 at the end of a round without casting, a full shift of meditation −3, a scene of talk or shared food with allies −1 (real care −2), a night in a dry room by a fire −1. At 10 neither food nor talk works: only the save, or an ally who brings the hero back with a Persuasion or Animal Ken check. Full rules are in `05-magic-psychosis.md`.

    Aura

    Aura is the world's opinion of you, worn where it shows: a track from −5 to +5, starting at 0. It is not spent and it is not a resource. It moves when you do something the world notices.

    • Spare a beaten foe, heal a stranger, keep a promise: aura rises one step.
    • Kill the defenceless, abandon a companion: aura falls one step.
    • Break a sworn pact: aura falls two steps.
    Light aura (+1 to +5)

    +1 die to Animal Ken and to Persuasion with beasts, tribes and settlers who have heard of you. Animals come closer instead of running.

    Dark aura (−1 to −5)

    +1 die to Brawl and Shooting against an enemy who has already seen you draw blood. −1 die to Animal Ken: beasts remember you.

    Aura changes after the action that caused it, never during. It can be read by anyone who meets the character for more than a few minutes, and the master may use it to decide how a tribe opens the gate.

    07

    Creating a mutant

    Creation is seven steps, and the first four decide almost everything about how the hero will feel at the table.

    1. Lineage. Choose one. Take its free skill step and its trait.
    2. Attributes. Fourteen points, 2 to 5 each, in the classic variant; or all four at C with three increases in the modern one — exactly as the player's book says.
    3. Skills. Ten points to place. No skill above 3 in the classic variant or above B in the modern one. At least three skills should stay at 0: a mutant who can do everything has not decided what they are.
    4. Mutations. Two bio-mutations, or one bio and one psi. Psi-mutations begin at grade I.
    5. Second trait. One more from the beast traits chapter.
    6. Aura, tribe, name. Aura starts at 0. Name the tribe that raised the hero and the one thing they left behind.
    7. Gear. A weapon in each hand or one two-handed weapon, armour if the tribe has any, and eight regular items. Load is twice Strength in the classic variant, or the Strength die's size in the modern one — a backpack doubles it at the price of −2 on Acrobatics checks.

    Mutations are grown, not found: raising one takes a week of fever, hunger and sleep in a safe place, and the master may ask for an Endurance roll at the end of it. A failed roll does not lose the experience — the change simply takes another week.

    A mutant in one line

    Pick what the body became, pick what the mind can push out of it, and spend everything else on the skills that keep the tribe fed.
