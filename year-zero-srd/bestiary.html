    Root & Claw · Bestiary

    Bestiary of the Overgrowth

    What people meet between the fires: pests, hunters, things grown out of root and sap, the shapes the rot leaves walking, the great ones, and the people a party meets on the road. Every entry is a card a master can run from — threat, numbers, what it does, what it wants and what can be taken from it.

    00

    How to read a beast

    Threat is not level: it says what the thing costs if the party is careless. ● is one hero's bad round. ●● is a real fight for a starting group. ●●● is the centre of a session, and should have a place, a weakness and a way out.

    The numbers are classic: four characteristics, health, armour. Attacks give damage and range. In the modern variant read the characteristic with the table below and keep health and damage as numbers.

    What it wants is the most useful line on the card. A beast that gets what it wants usually leaves; a beast that cannot get it fights to the death. Rot-touched never break and never leave.

    At the table: one card and one initiative draw for a group of identical beasts. Give a group +1 die to attacks for every beast past the first, up to +3. A beast at half health runs, unless the card says otherwise. A rot save is an Endurance roll; failure means the Blush (see the lore).

    Scale. Three threat 1 things are a threat 2 encounter; a threat 3 with two threat 1 servants is a session. Do not add numbers to a great thing — add terrain.

    01

    Scavengers and pests

    What lives off a town rather than in it: the things that eat cable, blood and light. More nuisance than danger, until there are nine of them.

    Wire rat — the cable chewer

    Threat●Str2Agl4Wit1Emp1HP2Armour0

    Attacks. Bite 1 (Engaged).

    Feature. Chews cable, rope and straps, and leaves copper dust where it has been. +1 die to Stealth in ruins.

    Wants. Warmth and copper.

    Loot. D6 ring-bits in its nest.

    Sap tick — a thirst with legs

    Threat●Str1Agl3Wit1Emp1HP1Armour1

    Attacks. Bite 1 and latch on.

    Feature. Latched: −1 die to Agility until it is pulled off (Force, fast action); drains 1 damage each round.

    Wants. Sap, and anything that has drunk it.

    Loot. A vial of thin sap, if the tick is squeezed.

    Ash moth — a grey cloud with a purpose

    Threat●Str1Agl4Wit1Emp1HP3Armour0

    Attacks. Wings 1 (Engaged).

    Feature. A swarm: single-target attacks inflict half damage, rounded up. In dim light it gives −1 die to Observation.

    Wants. Light, and the warmth of a lantern.

    Loot. A pinch of grey dust; Crafting makes a smoke bomb from it.

    Copper crow — the thief with a memory

    Threat●Str1Agl5Wit2Emp2HP2Armour0

    Attacks. Peck 1 (Engaged).

    Feature. Steals small items and mimics voices; +2 dice to Stealth in flight. It remembers a face that cheated it.

    Wants. Anything that shines.

    Loot. Whatever it stole last — often a courier's token.

    02

    Predators

    The Overgrowth's hunters are not evil and not stupid. Each has a way of taking one hero out of the fight, and each would rather not be hurt.

    Long-jaw — the patient hunter

    Threat●●Str4Agl4Wit2Emp1HP6Armour1

    Attacks. Claws 2 (Engaged); bite 3 (Engaged).

    Feature. Pounce: after moving a zone straight at a target, the first attack deals +2 damage. +1 die to Stealth in tall growth.

    Wants. One straggler, separated from the fire.

    Loot. Pelt (armour 1 when worked), teeth.

    Wild tusk boar — the argument with legs

    Threat●●Str5Agl3Wit1Emp1HP8Armour2

    Attacks. Gore 3 (Engaged); charge +2.

    Feature. Charge in a straight line for +2 damage; a struck target falls prone unless it wins an Acrobatics roll.

    Wants. To be left alone, and the best roots in the marsh.

    Loot. Tusk, hide, a week of meat.

    Ridge hawk — the shadow that falls

    Threat●●Str2Agl5Wit2Emp1HP3Armour0

    Attacks. Talons 2 (Engaged).

    Feature. Dive: +2 damage when it attacks from above. Animal Ken trains one in a season; Ridgewatch sells the chicks.

    Wants. Small prey, and a high perch.

    Loot. Feathers for fletching; an aerie holds bones and lost tools.

    Grey packhound — the one that is never alone

    Threat●●Str3Agl4Wit2Emp2HP4Armour1

    Attacks. Bite 2 (Engaged).

    Feature. Pack: +1 die for every other hound attacking the same target this round, up to +3. A howl brings D3 more in a stretch.

    Wants. The pack's prey, and to keep the pack fed.

    Loot. Pelt; sometimes a courier's collar still around its neck.

    03

    Root and sap things

    Root and sap made animals that should not work. They are slower than a predator, harder to kill, and they do not lose interest.

    Sap hound — grown wrong and fast

    Threat●●Str3Agl4Wit1Emp1HP5Armour1

    Attacks. Claws 2 (Engaged); acid spit 1 (Short).

    Feature. The spit ignores armour on a second success. It tracks by sap scent and never loses a wounded target.

    Wants. Sap, and the warm bodies that carry it.

    Loot. A hardened sap gland; Crafting makes a blade coating from it.

    Root worm — the ground that moves

    Threat●●Str4Agl2Wit1Emp1HP7Armour2

    Attacks. Bite 3 (Engaged); constrict 2.

    Feature. Burrows; senses tremors two zones away. Surfaces under a target: Acrobatics or fall prone. Fire drives it back.

    Wants. Warmth — a fire, a burrow, a sleeping camp.

    Loot. A segment of root-iron; the Latch pays well for it.

    Glass wasp — a wing you hear too late

    Threat●●Str1Agl5Wit1Emp1HP2Armour1

    Attacks. Sting 2 (Engaged) and venom.

    Feature. Venom: Endurance or −2 dice on all rolls for a stretch. Flies; a nest holds D6 wasps.

    Wants. Meat for the nest.

    Loot. A glass wing, prized by crafters; the nest holds a queen's amber.

    Spore shepherd — it keeps a flock you cannot see

    Threat●●Str3Agl2Wit3Emp2HP5Armour1

    Attacks. Staff 2 (Engaged).

    Feature. Exhales spores: everyone at Engaged loses a fast action unless they win an Endurance roll; failure also gives −2 for a round. Herds rot-touched beasts.

    Wants. To drive its flock into a village.

    Loot. A spore horn; sounding it calls the flock.

    04

    The rot-touched

    The rot does not make monsters. It makes the sick keep walking. These are the three shapes a village fears most.

    Bloom stag — beautiful, and already gone

    Threat●●Str4Agl4Wit2Emp1HP6Armour1

    Attacks. Antlers 3 (Engaged).

    Feature. In the Bloom stage. On death it releases spores: everyone at Engaged takes −2 for a stretch unless they win an Endurance roll.

    Wants. To reach the heartwood before it stops walking.

    Loot. Antlers for Crafting; the spores are worth money and are not safe to carry.

    Blush bear — the fever that stands up

    Threat●●●Str5Agl3Wit2Emp1HP10Armour2

    Attacks. Claws 3 (Engaged); bite 3 (Engaged).

    Feature. Ignores the first critical injury it suffers. In the Blush stage its blood is contagious: anyone cut by it must save against the rot.

    Wants. A den, food, and to be left to the fever.

    Loot. Pelt (armour 2 when worked); the meat is tainted.

    Garden saint — it still speaks, and that is the trap

    Threat●●●Str3Agl2Wit4Emp3HP7Armour1

    Attacks. Touch 2 (Engaged) and spores.

    Feature. In the Garden stage, but still talking. Its touch forces a rot save. It commands rot-touched beasts and cannot be persuaded to stop — only delayed with a good argument.

    Wants. A congregation.

    Loot. A hymnal of the Ash Choir; a dry-room key.

    05

    Great things

    One card each, and a whole session around each: these are not encounters, they are weather with an opinion.

    Root-eater — the wall that chews back

    Threat●●●Str6Agl2Wit1Emp1HP14Armour3

    Attacks. Bite 4 (Engaged); sweep 3 (all at Engaged).

    Feature. Eats through a wall in a stretch; senses tremors three zones away. Only fire and loud machines turn it aside.

    Wants. The oldest root it can smell.

    Loot. A plate of root-iron; a cart-load if the body is butchered.

    Canopy drifter — a jellyfish with a sky

    Threat●●●Str3Agl5Wit1Emp1HP8Armour1

    Attacks. Tentacles 2 twice (Engaged).

    Feature. Drifts between zones and never takes falling damage. In dim light it is nearly invisible: −2 to Observation to notice it before it is Engaged.

    Wants. To drift, and to sting what it touches.

    Loot. A gas sac; the glide membrane graft is grown from it.

    Mire mother — the marsh that keeps its children

    Threat●●●Str4Agl2Wit3Emp2HP9Armour2

    Attacks. Grab 2 (Engaged); drown.

    Feature. Drags a grabbed target into the water; heals 1 damage per round while in the marsh. Her lair holds D6 young, each a threat 1 beast.

    Wants. Offerings, and to be left her stretch of water.

    Loot. An amber heart, a fine psi focus; the young are worth more alive.

    Old Tusk — the one the clans will not fight

    Threat●●●Str6Agl4Wit3Emp3HP12Armour3

    Attacks. Tusks 4 (Engaged); trample 3.

    Feature. Speaks a broken trade tongue. The Tusk Line will not raise a hand against it. +1 die against anyone who has harmed a boar-kin.

    Wants. The old paths kept open and the marsh-holds unburned.

    Loot. Nothing. Killing it is a curse, and every boar-kin will know.

    06

    People of the Overgrowth

    Not monsters: the people a party meets on the road, at a gate, in a dry room. Give each a want and a reason not to fight, and the world runs itself.

    Ridgewatch lookout — sells the warning, not the fight

    Threat●Str2Agl4Wit3Emp2HP4Armour1

    Attacks. Spear 2 (Engaged); sling 1 (Medium).

    Feature. Sees a zone further than anyone: +2 dice to Observation. A watch-call brings D3 more in a stretch. Will not fight on the ground if it can climb.

    Wants. To sell the warning before the thing arrives.

    Loot. Rings, a brass whistle, a map of other people's aeries.

    Long Run courier — carries it or dies with it

    Threat●Str2Agl5Wit2Emp2HP4Armour0

    Attacks. Knife 1 (Engaged); staff 2 (Engaged).

    Feature. +1 zone per fast action and knows the root roads. Carries a sealed token and will die before opening it.

    Wants. To deliver, and to be owed a favour.

    Loot. A sealed token, D6 rings, a list of routes.

    Sap tapper — a little wrong, and useful

    Threat●Str3Agl2Wit3Emp1HP5Armour0

    Attacks. Tap-knife 2 (Engaged).

    Feature. Reads sap: knows whether a wound, a well or a person is tainted. Drinks small doses and is a little wrong because of it.

    Wants. A clean vein and a buyer who does not ask.

    Loot. Tapping tools, a flask of raw sap, a tainted hand.

    Slow Court clerk — the debt remembers

    Threat●Str1Agl2Wit4Emp3HP3Armour0

    Attacks. None worth rolling; a guard does that.

    Feature. Remembers every debt in the valley; a receipt in their hand is law. Cannot be bribed with rings — only with a debt.

    Wants. The account balanced and the precedent kept.

    Loot. A ledger, a seal, D6 filed claims.

    Latch breaker — paid for the door, not the room

    Threat●●Str3Agl4Wit3Emp2HP4Armour1

    Attacks. Crowbar 2 (Engaged); thrown spanner 1 (Short).

    Feature. Opens a lock or a machine in a stretch with the right tools: +1 die to Crafting. Runs if the job goes loud.

    Wants. To be paid for the door, not for what is behind it.

    Loot. Tools, a copied key, a pump diagram.

    Ash Choir singer — mercy with a voice

    Threat●●Str2Agl2Wit3Emp4HP4Armour0

    Attacks. Staff 1 (Engaged); spores.

    Feature. Exhales spores at Engaged: Endurance or lose a fast action and take −2 for a round. Truly believes the Garden is mercy, and is hard to hate in person.

    Wants. One more who will listen.

    Loot. A hymnal, a spore horn, a dry-room key that is not theirs.

    Marsh guide — knows which water is sap

    Threat●●Str3Agl3Wit3Emp2HP5Armour0

    Attacks. Pole 2 (Engaged); knife 2 (Engaged).

    Feature. Knows which water is sap and which path holds weight: the party does not roll for the marsh while they lead. Paid in advance, always.

    Wants. To finish the crossing and be owed nothing.

    Loot. A pole, a sap-proof cloak, a purse of rings.

    Pack mercenary — the contract, not the cause

    Threat●●Str4Agl3Wit2Emp2HP7Armour2

    Attacks. Axe 3 (Engaged); crossbow 2 (Medium).

    Feature. Fights in a line and does not break while another of the Pack stands: +1 die while an ally is Engaged with the same foe. Will not take a contract against the Pack.

    Wants. The contract kept and the pack paid.

    Loot. Axe, crossbow, half the pay in advance.
