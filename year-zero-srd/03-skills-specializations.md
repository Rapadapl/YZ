# Root & Claw — Skills and Specializations

## Twelve Basic Skills

### Skill Descriptions

1. **Skelm (Body)** - Lifting, moving, or breaking heavy/tough obstacles
2. **Near Combat (Body)** - Attacking or defending with cold weapons or unarmed
3. **Endurance (Body)** - Stamina, e.g., holding breath underwater, resisting poison
4. **Ranged (Dexterity)** - Shooting with ranged weapons
5. **Dexterity (Dexterity)** - Risky movements and precise actions (jumping, climbing, escaping)
6. **Stealth (Dexterity)** - Hiding unnoticed, stealing
7. **Craft (Wits)** - Repairing damaged items, creating new ones
8. **Observation (Wits)** - Studying terrain, gaining useful information, noticing things
9. **Survival (Wits)** - Surviving extreme temperatures, acid rain, sandstorms, etc.
10. **Healing (Empathy)** - Healing injured characters, saving lives
11. **Insight (Empathy)** - Understanding others, detecting lies
12. **Persuasion (Empathy)** - Hypnotizing, convincing others

## Skill Checks

### Classic Variant

When a character makes a skill check, take dice equal to the sum of the skill level and the associated characteristic value. Roll all dice simultaneously.

- **Success**: If at least one die shows a 6 - the action succeeds
- **Multiple successes**: Each 6 rolled is a success
- **No skill?**: If the character doesn't have the required skill, roll only characteristic dice
- **Equipment**: Add extra dice if helpful equipment is available

### Modern Variant

When making a skill check, take 2 dice: one corresponding to the skill rank and one to the associated characteristic rank. Roll both dice simultaneously.

- **Success**: If at least one die shows 6 or higher - the action succeeds
- **Two successes**: If a 10+ is rolled (only possible with d10 and d12), it counts as 2 successes
- **No skill?**: If the character doesn't have the skill, roll only the characteristic die
- **Equipment**: Helpful equipment can give positive modifiers or add extra dice

### Successes and Effects

- If multiple successes are rolled, the hero not only achieves the goal but also gets an additional effect (at gamemaster's discretion):
  - Hero acts quickly
  - Hero doesn't make noise
  - Hero helps a companion
  - Hero impresses someone
  - Hero discovers something unexpected

- 3+ successes mainly matter in combat, where each additional success increases damage by 1 point

### Critical Failure

- If no successes are rolled on a skill check, something went wrong
- Describe why it failed and consult gamemaster for additional consequences
- Must be able to move forward - at cost of additional effort, time, or other resources

## Special Effort

When a hero fully commits to a task at risk of failure, they can make a special effort to re-roll any dice that didn't show a 1. 1s on main dice are called "curses" - they are not re-rolled.

### Modern Variant - Wounds and Stress

After special effort, character takes 1 wound point (if skill linked to Body or Dexterity) or 1 stress point (if linked to Wits or Empathy) for each curse rolled.

### Characteristic Reduction

After each curse, immediately reduce the used characteristic (only main characteristic dice, not skill or equipment).

### Kubik Stressa (Stress Dice)

After special effort, immediately gain 1 stress point per curse; these d6 dice add to skill checks (6s are successes), but if a 1 appears on a stress die, must make a panic check according to the panic table.

### Conditions

After special effort, receive a condition (e.g., exhausted, annoyed, frightened); each imposes penalties on certain skill checks.

### The World Answers

There is no separate currency for bad luck. For each curse on a special effort, the gamemaster may answer at once or later with a complication that fits the fiction: a ledge gives way, a witness sees too much, a tool jams, a patrol arrives. The size of the complication matches the number of curses, and it is played out as an ordinary event, not spent from a pool.

### Only Once

Special effort can only be applied once per check.

## Advantages and Penalties

### Modern Variant Advantages/Penalties

- **Advantage**: If hero has advantage, add a third main die corresponding to the minimum of characteristic and skill ranks. Successes on the third die are added to the others.
- **Penalty**: If hero has penalty, remove the main die with fewest faces (so the check is performed with only one die, not two).
- **Advantage and penalty cancel each other out**
- After all cancellations, only one modifier can remain on the check

### Classic Variant Modifiers

- +1 adds 1 main die; +2 adds 2 main dice, etc.
- -1 means rolling 1 main die less, etc.
- Multiple modifiers stack
- Prefer adding/removing dice related to the skill characteristic, then equipment, then main characteristic dice
- If a skill die needs to be removed but none exist - remove equipment die; if equipment dice are exhausted - remove main characteristic dice
- If no dice remain, chances of success are none - time to think of another plan!

### Help (Modern)

- Other players or gamemaster can help
- Must be declared before rolling
- Helper must be nearby and able to influence the situation
- Each helper provides +1 modifier
- Maximum 3 helpers, so max +3
- In combat, helping costs the same type of action (quick or standard) as the assisted character

## Opposed Checks

- Sometimes one success isn't enough
- Must get more successes than opponent
- Each opponent's success cancels one of your successes
- Only the initiator of opposed check can make special effort, and only after opponent's check
- Can be different skills (e.g., Persuasion vs. Insight) or same skill

### Tie

- If opponents get equal number of successes - it's a tie
- Usually the side that initiated the conflict loses, but sometimes tie has special consequences
- If tie needs resolution - conduct opposed check again

## Skill Examples

### Skelm (Body)
- Required when hero needs to lift, move, or break something heavy or tough blocking the path
- Use when demonstrating notable strength

### Near Combat (Body)
- When character attacks opponent or defends with cold weapons or unarmed
- Detailed combat rules in Chapter 4

### Endurance (Body)
- When hero's stamina or toughness is in question
- Example: holding breath underwater, resisting poison
- Successful check can prevent death from fatal injury

### Ranged (Dexterity)
- Used for shooting with any ranged weapon
- Detailed combat rules in Chapter 4

### Dexterity (Dexterity)
- When hero's actions require dexterity and precision
- Example: risky climb, dangerous jump, escaping from enemy (see Chase rules)

### Stealth (Dexterity)
- When hero tries to hide unnoticed or steal something
- If opponent is vigilant and actively searching - opposed check vs. Observation
- Without vigilant opponent - simple stealth check
- +1 modifier for: blending with surroundings, darkness/twilight, hiding in crowd or noise
- -1 modifier for: standing out from surroundings, no place to hide

### Craft (Wits)
- Primarily needed to repair damaged items and create new ones
- Repairing usually takes a full shift
- Other applications depend on game world

### Observation (Wits)
- When need to study terrain to gain useful information or notice something
- Gamemaster can give positive modifier (or even automatic success) if player specifies exactly where character is looking

### Survival (Wits)
- When need to not die in extreme temperatures, acid rain, sandstorms, or other weather unpleasantness
- Details in Chapter 6

### Healing (Empathy)
- When characters get injured; used to get injured characters back on their feet and save lives
- Details in combat and injury rules

### Insight (Empathy)
- Understand others and detect lies
- On success, gamemaster must describe strongest emotion of target: hate, fear, contempt, love, etc.
- With more than 1 success, can understand if target is lying
- Also needed to oppose another's Persuasion check

### Persuasion (Empathy)
- Instead of resorting to violence, can outsmart or convince opponent
- Must define stake - what hero wants to achieve
- Goal must be within reason: no one agrees to act directly against their interests or do everything that comes to mind
- Final decision whether check is needed is up to gamemaster

## Group Persuasion

- Usually address group leader or representative
- Other factors can give +1 or -1 modifiers per list in document
