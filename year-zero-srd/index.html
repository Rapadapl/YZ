ROOT & CLAW

Animals stood up after the Tree. Now they carry the world on their backs.

Root & Claw is a mutant-animal module: a lineage instead of a species, twelve skills, two kinds of mutation, an aura the world reads before you speak. The module sits on top of the core rules; the core books sit beside it.
