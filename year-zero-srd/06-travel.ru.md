# Year Zero Engine - Путешествия (Русский)

## Карта и секторы

### Картographic basics
- Map divided into sectors for travel tracking
- Each sector represents defined area (typically several kilometers)
- Overland travel uses sector-based movement
- Terrain type affects movement cost and encounter chances

### Sector types
- **Clear**: Easy travel, normal speed, low encounter chance
- **Forest**: Reduced speed, higher encounter chance, navigation checks
- **Mountains**: Significant speed reduction, climbing checks
- **Water**: Travel by river/lake different rules
- **Urban**: City travel, different encounter mechanics

## Задачи путешествия

### Journey tasks
- Navigate through unknown territory
- Manage resources (food, water, supplies)
- Avoid or handle random encounters
- Handle terrain challenges
- Reach destination within time limit

### Random encounters
- Determined by terrain, season, and region
- Can be friendly, neutral, or hostile
- Resolution through combat, negotiation, or avoidance

## Движение

### Movement rates
- **Walking**: Base movement rate per period
- **Hiking**: Forced march, increased speed at cost
- **Transport**: Animals, vehicles have different rates
- **Forced march**: Can push beyond normal limits with risk

### Terrain effects
- Each terrain type has movement modifier
- Some require skill checks (Survival, Navigation)
- Difficult terrain may halve movement rate
- Forced marches increase exhaustion risk

## Случайные встречи

### Encounter determination
- Based on region, terrain, season, time of day
- Encounter tables provide variety
- Can be adjusted for campaign needs

### Encounter types
- **Hostile**: Creatures, bandits, dangers
- **Neutral**: Traders, travelers, wildlife
- **Friendly**: Helpful NPCs, settlements
- **Environmental**: Weather, terrain hazards

### Resolution
- Combat if conflict arises
- Negotiation or parlay
- Avoidance through stealth or speed
- Parley with leaders

## Типы местности

### Terrain categories
- **Open plains**: Fast travel, visible, weather exposed
- **Forest**: Reduced speed, cover, navigation needed
- **Hills**: Moderate speed, some elevation changes
- **Mountains**: Slow travel, climbing checks
- **Water**: River, lake, sea travel rules
- **Urban**: City streets, building navigation

### Specific modifiers
- Forest: -1 to movement, +1 encounter check
- Mountain: -2 to movement, climbing checks required
- Water: Different rules based on vessel type
- Urban: Complex navigation, encounter modifiers

## Погода

### Weather system
- Affects travel speed, comfort, survival checks
- Seasonal patterns and variations
- Can change during journey

### Weather effects
- **Clear**: Normal travel, no penalties
- **Rain**: Reduced speed, uncomfortable, skill checks possible
- **Snow**: Significant speed reduction, cold damage risk
- **Storm**: Hazardous travel, possible shelter needed
- **Extreme**: Dangerous, may force stop or route change

### Survival checks
- Required in harsh weather conditions
- Failure can result in damage, exhaustion, or delayed travel
- Proper gear can reduce penalties

## Неприятности

### Hiking unpleasantries
- **Thirst**: Must make consumption checks
- **Hunger**: Food tracking and starvation rules
- **Fatigue**: Accumulated exhaustion over time
- **Cold/Heat**: Temperature-related checks

### Camp unpleasantness
- Nighttime comfort checks
- Watch schedules and surprise risks
- Camp security measures
- Weather protection needs
