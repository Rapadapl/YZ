# Bestiary of the Overgrowth

What people meet between the fires: pests, hunters, things grown out of root and sap, the shapes the rot leaves walking, the great ones, and the people a party meets on the road. Every entry is a card a master can run from — threat, numbers, what it does, what it wants and what can be taken from it.

## How to read a beast

<strong>Threat</strong> is not level: it says what the thing costs if the party is careless. ● is one hero's bad round. ●● is a real fight for a starting group. ●●● is the centre of a session, and should have a place, a weakness and a way out.

<strong>The numbers</strong> are classic: four characteristics, health, armour. Attacks give damage and range. <strong>HP is wounds</strong>: that many boxes before the beast is taken out; a hero or a human ally has 10. In the modern variant read the characteristic with the table below and keep health and damage as numbers.

| Classic | Rank |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | D |
| 3–4 | C |
| 5 | B |
| 6 | A |

<strong>What it wants</strong> is the most useful line on the card. A beast that gets what it wants usually leaves; a beast that cannot get it fights to the death. Rot-touched never break and never leave.

<strong>At the table:</strong> one card and one initiative draw for a group of identical beasts. Give a group +1 die to attacks for every beast past the first, up to +3. A beast at half health runs, unless the card says otherwise. A rot save is an Endurance roll; failure means the Blush (see <a href="lore.html">the lore</a>).

<strong>Mutations.</strong> Many beasts here already carry a mutation, noted under "Feature" and folded into the card's numbers. If you need a new beast, apply a mutation to any card using the rules in section 07; the same place lists what can be harvested and what it costs.

<strong>Scale.</strong> Three threat 1 things are a threat 2 encounter; a threat 3 with two threat 1 servants is a session. Do not add numbers to a great thing — add terrain.

## 01 — Scavengers and pests

What lives off a town rather than in it: the things that eat cable, blood and light. More nuisance than danger, until there are nine of them.

### Wire rat — the cable chewer

- **Threat:** ● · Str 2 · Agl 4 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 2 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Bite 1 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Chews cable, rope and straps, and leaves copper dust where it has been. +1 die to Stealth in ruins.
- **Wants:** Warmth and copper.
- **Loot:** D6 ring-bits in its nest.

### Sap tick — a thirst with legs

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 3 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 1 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Bite 1 and latch on.
- **Feature:** Latched: −1 die to Agility until it is pulled off (Force, fast action); drains 1 damage each round.
- **Wants:** Sap, and anything that has drunk it.
- **Loot:** A vial of thin sap, if the tick is squeezed.

### Ash moth — a grey cloud with a purpose

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 4 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 3 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Wings 1 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** A swarm: single-target attacks inflict half damage, rounded up. In dim light it gives −1 die to Observation.
- **Wants:** Light, and the warmth of a lantern.
- **Loot:** A pinch of grey dust; Crafting makes a smoke bomb from it.

### Copper crow — the thief with a memory

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 5 · Wit 2 · Emp 2 · HP 2 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Peck 1 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Steals small items and mimics voices; +2 dice to Stealth in flight. It remembers a face that cheated it.
- **Wants:** Anything that shines.
- **Loot:** Whatever it stole last — often a courier's token.


### Blind woodlouse — a shell that walks the wall

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 3 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 2 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Mandibles 1 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Mutation "Bone plates I" is already in its armour. Climbs walls and ceilings; +2 dice to Stealth in a cramped zone.
- **Wants:** Damp, carrion and warm stone.
- **Loot:** Shell plates — stock for armour 1.

### Bone hedgehog — a ball that strikes first

- **Threat:** ● · Str 2 · Agl 2 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 3 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Spines 1 (Engaged). Anyone striking it at Engaged range with a fist or a short weapon takes 1 damage.
- **Feature:** Mutation "Bone plates II". It curls up: Armour 3, but it cannot move or attack until it unrolls (fast action).
- **Wants:** Carrion, warmth and to be left alone.
- **Loot:** Spines (arrowheads), shell for armour 1–2.

### Resin slug — a puddle with an opinion

- **Threat:** ● · Str 2 · Agl 1 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 4 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Touch 1 and resin (Agility, or −1 die and movement costs a slow action until the resin is scraped off).
- **Feature:** Mutation "Mucus bubble I": it crawls on any surface, including ceilings. Fire drives it off; in water it is faster (Agl 3).
- **Wants:** Dark cellars, water and anything that will stick.
- **Loot:** Resin — sealant, fuel and trap-making stock.

### Lamprey-lantern — light that drinks

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 4 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 2 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Bite 1 and latch on.
- **Feature:** It glows in the dark: +2 to Observation to notice it, but −1 to its Stealth. Latched, it drains 1 wound per round and puts out a lantern (Agility, or the light dies for the rest of the scene).
- **Wants:** Blood and light.
- **Loot:** A glowing gland — a lantern for one shift with no flame.

## 02 — Predators

The Overgrowth's hunters are not evil and not stupid. Each has a way of taking one hero out of the fight, and each would rather not be hurt.

### Long-jaw — the patient hunter

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 4 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 6 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Claws 2 (Engaged); bite 3 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Pounce: after moving a zone straight at a target, the first attack deals +2 damage. +1 die to Stealth in tall growth.
- **Wants:** One straggler, separated from the fire.
- **Loot:** Pelt (armour 1 when worked), teeth.

### Wild tusk boar — the argument with legs

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 5 · Agl 3 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 8 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Gore 3 (Engaged); charge +2.
- **Feature:** Charge in a straight line for +2 damage; a struck target falls prone unless it wins an Acrobatics roll.
- **Wants:** To be left alone, and the best roots in the marsh.
- **Loot:** Tusk, hide, a week of meat.

### Ridge hawk — the shadow that falls

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 2 · Agl 5 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 3 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Talons 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Dive: +2 damage when it attacks from above. Animal Ken trains one in a season; Ridgewatch sells the chicks.
- **Wants:** Small prey, and a high perch.
- **Loot:** Feathers for fletching; an aerie holds bones and lost tools.

### Grey packhound — the one that is never alone

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 4 · Wit 2 · Emp 2 · HP 4 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Bite 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Pack: +1 die for every other hound attacking the same target this round, up to +3. A howl brings D3 more in a stretch.
- **Wants:** The pack's prey, and to keep the pack fed.
- **Loot:** Pelt; sometimes a courier's collar still around its neck.


### Rust lynx — a hunter of metal

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 5 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 5 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Claws 2 (Engaged); bite 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Mutations "Claws II" and "Night eyes I". A pounce from ambush adds +2 damage to its first attack. +2 dice to Stealth in rusty growth.
- **Wants:** Small prey and a high place over a trail.
- **Loot:** Hide (armour 1), claws, sometimes a copper collar.

### Two-headed otter — two heads, one hunt

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 4 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 6 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Bite 2 (Engaged). The second head can bite again as a fast action.
- **Feature:** One head sleeps while the other watches: +2 dice against a surprise attack. In water, +1 die to all attacks and Agility checks.
- **Wants:** Fish, warm burrows and both heads fed.
- **Loot:** Hide (armour 1), two skulls — good trade for the Slow Court.

### Thorn badger — a home that bites

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 2 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 7 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Claws 2 (Engaged); bite 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Mutation "Bone plates II" is already in its armour. In its burrow it cannot be flanked, and only Force clears the entrance. It does not retreat while cubs are inside.
- **Wants:** Roots, grubs and an untouchable burrow.
- **Loot:** Hide (armour 2 once tanned), claws, D6 grubs.

### Night marten — what used to be on the table

- **Threat:** ● · Str 2 · Agl 5 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 3 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Bite 1 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Mutations "Soft paws" and "Gripping tail I": +2 dice to Stealth, climbs without a roll, carries off an item up to a child's weight. It remembers where it was fed, and comes back.
- **Wants:** Food, anything shiny, and a warm place in the rafters.
- **Loot:** Fur, stolen goods, sometimes a pantry key.

### Glass-beak shrike — thorns with feathers

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 5 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 2 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Beak 1 (Engaged); dive +2 damage.
- **Feature:** Mutation "Wing fold I": glides between zones. It impales prey on thorns and barbed wire; its nest holds 1–2 things it could not carry.
- **Wants:** Small prey and a high perch.
- **Loot:** Glass beak (a tool), feathers, someone else's belongings in the nest.

## 03 — Root and sap things

Root and sap made animals that should not work. They are slower than a predator, harder to kill, and they do not lose interest.

### Sap hound — grown wrong and fast

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 4 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 5 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Claws 2 (Engaged); acid spit 1 (Short).
- **Feature:** The spit ignores armour on a second success. It tracks by sap scent and never loses a wounded target.
- **Wants:** Sap, and the warm bodies that carry it.
- **Loot:** A hardened sap gland; Crafting makes a blade coating from it.

### Root worm — the ground that moves

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 2 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 7 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Bite 3 (Engaged); constrict 2.
- **Feature:** Burrows; senses tremors two zones away. Surfaces under a target: Acrobatics or fall prone. Fire drives it back.
- **Wants:** Warmth — a fire, a burrow, a sleeping camp.
- **Loot:** A segment of root-iron; the Latch pays well for it.

### Glass wasp — a wing you hear too late

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 1 · Agl 5 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 2 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Sting 2 (Engaged) and venom.
- **Feature:** Venom: Endurance or −2 dice on all rolls for a stretch. Flies; a nest holds D6 wasps.
- **Wants:** Meat for the nest.
- **Loot:** A glass wing, prized by crafters; the nest holds a queen's amber.

### Spore shepherd — it keeps a flock you cannot see

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 2 · Wit 3 · Emp 2 · HP 5 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Staff 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Exhales spores: everyone at Engaged loses a fast action unless they win an Endurance roll; failure also gives −2 for a round. Herds rot-touched beasts.
- **Wants:** To drive its flock into a village.
- **Loot:** A spore horn; sounding it calls the flock.


### Sap deer — a forest that walks

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 4 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 7 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Antlers 2 (Engaged); hooves 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Mutation "Regeneration I": heals 1 wound per shift, even without rest. It walks to a salt lick and will not turn aside; fire does not scare it, it angers it.
- **Wants:** A salt lick, water, and nobody standing on its trail.
- **Loot:** Antlers (Crafting), sap gland, hide.

### Root dog — the foundation someone chews

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 3 · Wit 2 · Emp 2 · HP 5 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Bite 2 (Engaged); scrape 1.
- **Feature:** Mutation "Termite jaws I": chews through a wooden door in a shift, stone in a day. Feels footsteps through the ground one zone away. Runs in packs of 1d3+1.
- **Wants:** To gnaw a base — of a house, a bridge, a root.
- **Loot:** Mandibles (Crafting), hide; tools of previous victims in the den.

### Trumpet fungus — a scream on a stalk

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 1 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 4 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Spores 2 (Short, everyone in the zone).
- **Feature:** Mutation "Spore sac I". It cannot move. If wounded or cut, it screams: everyone within two zones hears, and rot-touched beasts come to the sound within a shift.
- **Wants:** Damp, shade and quiet.
- **Loot:** Spore sac — a smoke bomb or a poison base.

### Resin bull — a ram with horns

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 5 · Agl 2 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 10 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Horns 3 (Engaged); charge +2 and knocks down (Agility, or prone).
- **Feature:** Mutation "Thick hide II" is already in its armour. In a resin pit it heals 1 wound per round; fire drives it off, but burning resin makes it worse (+1 damage, 1 wound per round).
- **Wants:** A salt lick, water and its stretch of trail.
- **Loot:** Hide (armour 2), horns, D6 measures of resin.

### Leaf wasp — a nest that hums

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 1 · Agl 5 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 3 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Sting 2 (Engaged) and venom; bite 1.
- **Feature:** Mutation "Wing fold I". Venom: Endurance, or −2 dice for a shift. A leaf nest holds 1d6 wasps; smoke quiets them, fire drives them out.
- **Wants:** Meat for larvae and a warm place under a roof.
- **Loot:** Comb honey, venom, leaf wax.

## 04 — The rot-touched

The rot does not make monsters. It makes the sick keep walking. These are the three shapes a village fears most.

### Bloom stag — beautiful, and already gone

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 4 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 6 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Antlers 3 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** In the Bloom stage. On death it releases spores: everyone at Engaged takes −2 for a stretch unless they win an Endurance roll.
- **Wants:** To reach the heartwood before it stops walking.
- **Loot:** Antlers for Crafting; the spores are worth money and are not safe to carry.

### Blush bear — the fever that stands up

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 5 · Agl 3 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 10 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Claws 3 (Engaged); bite 3 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Ignores the first critical injury it suffers. In the Blush stage its blood is contagious: anyone cut by it must save against the rot.
- **Wants:** A den, food, and to be left to the fever.
- **Loot:** Pelt (armour 2 when worked); the meat is tainted.

### Garden saint — it still speaks, and that is the trap

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 3 · Agl 2 · Wit 4 · Emp 3 · HP 7 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Touch 2 (Engaged) and spores.
- **Feature:** In the Garden stage, but still talking. Its touch forces a rot save. It commands rot-touched beasts and cannot be persuaded to stop — only delayed with a good argument.
- **Wants:** A congregation.
- **Loot:** A hymnal of the Ash Choir; a dry-room key.


### Blushing hare — a run that will not stop

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 5 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 3 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Bite 1 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Blush stage. It runs and does not stop until its breath gives out; it ignores difficult terrain while running. On death it releases spores: everyone Engaged makes an Endurance roll or catches the Blush.
- **Wants:** To reach the Garden while it still remembers the way.
- **Loot:** Nothing safe. The hide is infected; the spores are worth money and kill buyers.

### Rot shepherd — a flock nobody called

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 2 · Wit 3 · Emp 2 · HP 6 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Staff 2 (Engaged); spores 1 (Short).
- **Feature:** Drives 1d6 rot-touched beasts of threat 1; while it lives they do not flee the fight. Spores: everyone Engaged loses a fast action unless they win an Endurance roll.
- **Wants:** To drive the flock into a village and gain a new congregation.
- **Loot:** Spore horn, staff with a rot seal, D3 stolen tokens.

### Blooming fox — a beautiful end

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 2 · Agl 5 · Wit 3 · Emp 2 · HP 4 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Bite 2 (Engaged); scent 1 (Short, mental).
- **Feature:** Bloom stage. Scent: Wits, or the hero loses a fast action and follows the fox. It can lead a party into the thicket and come back alone.
- **Wants:** To be followed.
- **Loot:** Tail (a charm against the Rot for a scene), spores in the fur.

### Bone choir — a song from three throats

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 2 · Agl 2 · Wit 2 · Emp 4 · HP 5 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Voice 1 (Medium, mental); touch 1.
- **Feature:** It sings with three throats: everyone who hears gains 1 psychosis per round unless they win an Empathy roll. It cannot attack anyone singing with it. Rot-touched beasts gather at the song within a shift.
- **Wants:** Listeners and one more voice.
- **Loot:** Hymnal, three throat sacs (a focus for a psi-power).

### Wet saint — a baptism of Rot

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 4 · Agl 2 · Wit 4 · Emp 3 · HP 8 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Touch 2 (Engaged) and the Rot; staff 2.
- **Feature:** Garden stage. Its touch is a Rot save. It stands in water and does not leave it; in water it heals 1 wound per round. It speaks softly and offers to "wash away" whatever hurts.
- **Wants:** To baptise the party — all of them, one at a time.
- **Loot:** Key to a dry room, a book with wet pages, a vial of "holy" water (actually sap).

## 05 — Great things

One card each, and a whole session around each: these are not encounters, they are weather with an opinion.

### Root-eater — the wall that chews back

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 6 · Agl 2 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 14 · Armour 3
- **Attacks:** Bite 4 (Engaged); sweep 3 (all at Engaged).
- **Feature:** Eats through a wall in a stretch; senses tremors three zones away. Only fire and loud machines turn it aside.
- **Wants:** The oldest root it can smell.
- **Loot:** A plate of root-iron; a cart-load if the body is butchered.

### Canopy drifter — a jellyfish with a sky

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 3 · Agl 5 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 8 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Tentacles 2 twice (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Drifts between zones and never takes falling damage. In dim light it is nearly invisible: −2 to Observation to notice it before it is Engaged.
- **Wants:** To drift, and to sting what it touches.
- **Loot:** A gas sac; the glide membrane graft is grown from it.

### Mire mother — the marsh that keeps its children

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 4 · Agl 2 · Wit 3 · Emp 2 · HP 9 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Grab 2 (Engaged); drown.
- **Feature:** Drags a grabbed target into the water; heals 1 damage per round while in the marsh. Her lair holds D6 young, each a threat 1 beast.
- **Wants:** Offerings, and to be left her stretch of water.
- **Loot:** An amber heart, a fine psi focus; the young are worth more alive.

### Old Tusk — the one the clans will not fight

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 6 · Agl 4 · Wit 3 · Emp 3 · HP 12 · Armour 3
- **Attacks:** Tusks 4 (Engaged); trample 3.
- **Feature:** Speaks a broken trade tongue. The Tusk Line will not raise a hand against it. +1 die against anyone who has harmed a boar-kin.
- **Wants:** The old paths kept open and the marsh-holds unburned.
- **Loot:** Nothing. Killing it is a curse, and every boar-kin will know.


### Hollow mother — a home that digests

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 5 · Agl 1 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 12 · Armour 3
- **Attacks:** Roots 3 (everyone Engaged); bite 3 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** The hollow in her body is a dry room for one shift. Spores do not work inside, but the exit closes when she moves. 1d3 larvae of threat 1 come out of the hollow each scene.
- **Wants:** To stand in one place, eat and raise a brood.
- **Loot:** Heartwood (armour 3 as material), hollow honey, larvae.

### Resin whale — a river that breathes

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 6 · Agl 3 · Wit 1 · Emp 1 · HP 16 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Ram 4 (Engaged); tail 3 (everyone Engaged).
- **Feature:** It swims the resin rivers and surfaces in any zone that has resin. A resin fountain covers everyone Engaged (as the slug's resin). Fire angers it but does not stop it.
- **Wants:** Deep resin and quiet.
- **Loot:** Baleen, resin by the barrel, hide (armour 2, but heavy).

### Grandfather lichen — a memory that grows

- **Threat:** ●●● · Str 3 · Agl 1 · Wit 5 · Emp 3 · HP 10 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Touch 2 (Engaged); spores 1 (Short).
- **Feature:** Speaks an old tongue and remembers the names of the dead. Anyone who says his true name gets one answer. The lichen on him is a cure for the Rot, but taking it hurts: 1 wound.
- **Wants:** To hear his name said correctly.
- **Loot:** Lichen (medicine), a memory (the GM may give away one scene of the past).

## 06 — People of the Overgrowth

Not monsters: the people a party meets on the road, at a gate, in a dry room. Give each a want and a reason not to fight, and the world runs itself.

### Ridgewatch lookout — sells the warning, not the fight

- **Threat:** ● · Str 2 · Agl 4 · Wit 3 · Emp 2 · HP 4 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Spear 2 (Engaged); sling 1 (Medium).
- **Feature:** Sees a zone further than anyone: +2 dice to Observation. A watch-call brings D3 more in a stretch. Will not fight on the ground if it can climb.
- **Wants:** To sell the warning before the thing arrives.
- **Loot:** Rings, a brass whistle, a map of other people's aeries.

### Long Run courier — carries it or dies with it

- **Threat:** ● · Str 2 · Agl 5 · Wit 2 · Emp 2 · HP 4 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Knife 1 (Engaged); staff 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** +1 zone per fast action and knows the root roads. Carries a sealed token and will die before opening it.
- **Wants:** To deliver, and to be owed a favour.
- **Loot:** A sealed token, D6 rings, a list of routes.

### Sap tapper — a little wrong, and useful

- **Threat:** ● · Str 3 · Agl 2 · Wit 3 · Emp 1 · HP 5 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Tap-knife 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Reads sap: knows whether a wound, a well or a person is tainted. Drinks small doses and is a little wrong because of it.
- **Wants:** A clean vein and a buyer who does not ask.
- **Loot:** Tapping tools, a flask of raw sap, a tainted hand.

### Slow Court clerk — the debt remembers

- **Threat:** ● · Str 1 · Agl 2 · Wit 4 · Emp 3 · HP 3 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** None worth rolling; a guard does that.
- **Feature:** Remembers every debt in the valley; a receipt in their hand is law. Cannot be bribed with rings — only with a debt.
- **Wants:** The account balanced and the precedent kept.
- **Loot:** A ledger, a seal, D6 filed claims.

### Latch breaker — paid for the door, not the room

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 4 · Wit 3 · Emp 2 · HP 4 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Crowbar 2 (Engaged); thrown spanner 1 (Short).
- **Feature:** Opens a lock or a machine in a stretch with the right tools: +1 die to Crafting. Runs if the job goes loud.
- **Wants:** To be paid for the door, not for what is behind it.
- **Loot:** Tools, a copied key, a pump diagram.

### Ash Choir singer — mercy with a voice

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 2 · Agl 2 · Wit 3 · Emp 4 · HP 4 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Staff 1 (Engaged); spores.
- **Feature:** Exhales spores at Engaged: Endurance or lose a fast action and take −2 for a round. Truly believes the Garden is mercy, and is hard to hate in person.
- **Wants:** One more who will listen.
- **Loot:** A hymnal, a spore horn, a dry-room key that is not theirs.

### Marsh guide — knows which water is sap

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 3 · Wit 3 · Emp 2 · HP 5 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Pole 2 (Engaged); knife 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Knows which water is sap and which path holds weight: the party does not roll for the marsh while they lead. Paid in advance, always.
- **Wants:** To finish the crossing and be owed nothing.
- **Loot:** A pole, a sap-proof cloak, a purse of rings.

### Pack mercenary — the contract, not the cause

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 3 · Wit 2 · Emp 2 · HP 7 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Axe 3 (Engaged); crossbow 2 (Medium).
- **Feature:** Fights in a line and does not break while another of the Pack stands: +1 die while an ally is Engaged with the same foe. Will not take a contract against the Pack.
- **Wants:** The contract kept and the pack paid.
- **Loot:** Axe, crossbow, half the pay in advance.
### Sap healer — a clean vein

- **Threat:** ● · Str 2 · Agl 2 · Wit 3 · Emp 3 · HP 4 · Armour 0
- **Attacks:** Knife 1 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Reads sap: knows whether a wound, a well or a person is infected. Cures the Rot at an early stage, but takes payment in sap, not rings. Will not treat anyone who lies about the wound.
- **Wants:** A clean vein and a patient who does not lie.
- **Loot:** Tapping kit, flask of clean sap, D6 doses of medicine.

### Rot preacher — a garden that waits

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 3 · Agl 2 · Wit 3 · Emp 4 · HP 5 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Staff 2 (Engaged); spores 1 (Short).
- **Feature:** Preaches the mercy of the Garden and does not lie: he truly believes it. While he speaks, allies gain +1 die to Persuasion. Rot-touched beasts do not attack him.
- **Wants:** One more who will agree to listen to the end.
- **Loot:** Hymnal, cult seal, a list of names.

### Rot hunter — living merchandise

- **Threat:** ●● · Str 4 · Agl 3 · Wit 2 · Emp 1 · HP 6 · Armour 2
- **Attacks:** Net 1 (Short, entangles: Agility or two actions to get free); hook 2 (Engaged).
- **Feature:** Takes mutants alive: against an entangled target he attacks without penalty and deals no damage, dragging instead. He knows the price of every mutation and wastes no stock.
- **Wants:** A live specimen with a rare mutation.
- **Loot:** Net, hook, a commission token with the buyer's name.

### Mutant trader — a price for everything

- **Threat:** ● · Str 3 · Agl 3 · Wit 4 · Emp 3 · HP 4 · Armour 1
- **Attacks:** Knife 1 (Engaged); pistol 2 (Close).
- **Feature:** Buys mutations, sap and other people's secrets. Quotes the true price of loot (see "Mutations and loot") and does not lie about it — reputation is worth more. If a fight starts, he leaves rather than shoots.

## 07 — Mutations and loot

A mutated beast is not a separate species; it is a card plus one or two lines. Take any entry above, add a mutation from the module (or from the table below), and you have a new beast without a new card.

### Applying a mutation

- **One mutation** is an ordinary touched beast. **Two** is a rarity people talk about. **Three** is a legend or a GM mistake.
- Grade I changes one line: +1 armour, +1 damage, +1 die to one skill, +1 wound.
- Grade II changes two lines or one strong one: armour 2, +2 damage, +2 dice, +2 wounds.
- Grade III is a feature, not a number: flight, regrowing a limb, an aura, a swarm.
- A mutation does not make a beast smarter. It makes it more dangerous at what it already does.

**Example.** A Long Maw with "Bone plates II": HP 8, Armour 3, claws 2, bite 3, pounce +2. The same card, a different fight: a knife no longer solves it; fire and a trap do.

### Harvesting a mutant

A Crafting or Survival roll while butchering; each success is one find. Without a roll you get meat and hide, nothing else.

**Psi glands.** Sometimes a touched beast ripens a psi power instead of a bio-mutation: Spark, Silent Scream, Smell of Fear. Such a gland costs twice as much and is sold only into a dry room. The Lock and the Ash Choir both hunt for it.

**Careful.** Raw mutation takes root in a wound: anyone butchering without gloves who takes a wound makes an Endurance roll — or catches the Blush, as from sap.
