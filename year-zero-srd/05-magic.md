# Root & Claw — Magic

## Magic System Overview

Magic is a wild and unreliable force with countless manifestations. It is not studied in schools: knowledge passes from teacher to student within whichever magical tradition exists. This document describes seven traditions.

### Three Druidic Traditions (Nature-based)
- Healing
- Shapechange
- Divination

### Four Sorcerous Traditions (Power-based)
- Blood Magic
- Death Magic
- Symbolism
- Elementalism

Each tradition is usually associated with a specific specialization (see Chapter 3), without which casting spells of that tradition is impossible.

## Learning Magic

### Finding a Teacher

- Must find a teacher with higher rank in the required magical specialization than your character currently has
- May need to convince them and pay a substantial fee
- Without a teacher, improving or acquiring a magical specialization costs triple the experience points

### Spell Ranks

- All spells have rank from 1 to 3 (higher ranks may exist)
- If your character knows a magical specialization, they have access to all spells of their rank and lower
- To cast a spell one rank higher than your specialization allows is risky and always causes a random side effect
- Under no circumstances can a character cast a spell two or more ranks above their specialization

## Spell Creation

### Risky Magic

- Can create a spell one rank higher than specialization allows, but always causes a random side effect
- Never can cast a spell two or more ranks above specialization

### Casting costs nothing

- Magic has no resource: no points, no charges. A spell spends nothing but the caster.
- The price is **psychosis**: it rises from curses and shocks and at the top hands the body to the beast. Full rules in `05-magic-psychosis.md`.
- The number of casting dice equals the **spell's rank**: rank 1 is one die, rank 2 is two, rank 3 is three.
- Aura takes no part in a casting: it is the world's opinion, not a resource.

### Main power

- A spell's main power equals its rank.
- The number of main dice (d6) equals the spell's rank.
- Spell creation cannot fail.
- Each success (6) raises the final power by 1.
- Example: a rank 2 spell with two successes — final power 4.
- Special effort cannot be used when creating a spell.
- **Extra dice.** When a spell or a situation adds casting dice, each one adds 1 to the main power and raises the chance of a curse. It is not a resource: there is still nothing to pay but psychosis.

### Curses and side effects

- Every 1 rolled (a curse) adds psychosis: +1 for a rank 1 spell, +2 for rank 2, +3 for rank 3 and above. A psi-power of any grade: +1.
- If at least one curse is rolled, a side effect occurs: roll d12 on the table below.
- A curse does not cancel the spell: it may work and still leave a mark.
- At psychosis 10 the hero falls into instinct and at 18 transforms; the save is rolled with Empathy — see `05-magic-psychosis.md`.

### Safe Magic

- If creating a spell below the rank of your magical specialization, you may (but don't have to) roll one die less for each point of difference
- This reduces chance of side effect - and chance of enhancing spell
- If reduced to 0 or fewer dice - no check needed: spell works exactly as intended

### Distance

- Parameter determining maximum range at which spell effect can work
- "Self" means effect works only on those who created it

### Duration

- Parameter determining duration of spell effect
- **Instant**: Effect occurs immediately after which spell is considered completed

### Free casting

- A spell costs no points and no resource: nothing is spent but the caster's luck.
- The only price is psychosis from curses and shocks, see `05-magic-psychosis.md`

### Spellcasters and Gamemaster Characters

- Gamemaster characters have no resource: main power equals the spell's rank
- When one attempts to cast a spell, gamemaster can simply choose main power not exceeding corresponding advantage rank of player character
- Enhancement and side effects checks proceed normally

## Common Spells

### Magical Printing

- Allows protecting a reasonable creature or area (no larger than a human) from magic
- During shift, potency of any spells directed against protected target is reduced by magical printing's final power

### Sense Magic

- Gamemaster and druids can sense when someone uses magic within near distance without any check
- If character picks up an item, can determine if it's charged with magical energy
- To learn what specific charms are on an item - must use this spell; your spell's power must be equal or higher than magic being hidden

### Grimoires

- Not necessary to recite spell from book, but makes process easier and safer
- Therefore highly valued among magicians
- Spell rank from book or scroll considered one lower than normal
- In combat: must spend quick action to prepare grimoar

### Dispel Magic

- Can create interference with other mages' spells
- Response action; can disrupt turn order in combat (see "Rounds and Initiative" Chapter 4)
- Reduces opponent's spell potency by your dispel's potency
- If result reaches 0 (or drops below) - spell produces no effect
- Decide how many extra casting dice to roll for the dispelling before these checks
- Both you and opponent proceed with enhancement and side effects checks normally

### Hide Magic

- To cast a spell discreetly, add one casting die to hide it: that also raises the chance of a curse
- Does not count toward final power calculation
- To detect hidden magic, another mage must actively investigate area using Sense Magic
- To detect a hidden spell, another mage's Sense Magic power must be equal or higher than the hidden spell's final power

### Weave Words (Word of Power)

- Rank 1, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Your hero can create magical interference with other mages' spells. Response action; can disrupt turn order in combat.
- Reduces opponent's spell potency by your potency
- If result reaches 0 - spell produces no effect

### Conceal Magic

- Rank 2, Distance: self, Duration: instant
- To cast a spell discreetly, add one casting die to hide it: that also raises the chance of a curse
- Does not count toward final power calculation
- To detect hidden magic, other mage must actively investigate area using Sense Magic

### Suppress Magic

- Rank 2, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Reduces opponent's spell potency by your potency
- If result reaches 0 - spell produces no effect
- Decide how many extra casting dice to roll for the suppression before these checks

### Mask Magic

- Rank 2, Distance: self, Duration: instant
- Hide your magic
- For this does not count as action
- To detect hidden magic, other mage must actively investigate area using Sense Magic

### Resist Magic

- Rank 2, Duration: instant
- Can be used to resist opponent's spell
- Your potency vs. opponent's potency check

### Unraveling Magic

- Rank 2, Word of Power, Duration: instant
- Reduces opponent's spell potency by your potency
- If result reaches 0 - spell produces no effect
- Decide how many extra casting dice to roll for the dispelling before these checks

### Shielding Magic

- Rank 2, Distance: self, Duration: instant
- Hide your magic from detection
- For this does not count as action
- To detect hidden magic, other mage must actively investigate area using Sense Magic

### Beast's eye

- Rank 1, self, scene.
- See through the eyes of one beast at Short range. While it lasts, your own body is helpless.
- +1 die to Observation for whatever the beast sees.

### Quiet step

- Rank 1, self, scene.
- Your steps make no sound: +2 dice to Stealth while moving.
- The sound of your voice, a shot or a dropped object remains.

### Blood trace

- Rank 1, Engaged, instant.
- A touch stops the bleeding and gives +1 die to the next Healing check on that wound.
- It does nothing for a severed limb.

### Bark skin

- Rank 2, self, scene.
- The skin hardens: armour 2 against cutting and piercing.
- −1 die to Acrobatics while it lasts.

### Word of wind

- Rank 2, Short, instant.
- A gust strikes the target: an opposed Force roll, or the target is knocked down and loses a fast action.
- Fires in the zone are put out and smoke or dust is scattered.

### Death's shadow

- Rank 2, Short, a round.
- The target sees their own death: −2 dice on all rolls while it lasts.
- A second casting on the same target in the same scene does nothing.

### Bone spike

- Rank 3, Short, instant.
- A spike grows from earth or wall: damage 2, ignores 1 point of armour.
- Indoors it needs a surface a spike can grow from.

### Beast's gift

- Rank 3, Engaged, a shift.
- An ally gains one beast trait until the end of the scene.
- A second casting on the same hero in the same scene does nothing.

### Seal of silence

- Rank 3, Short, scene.
- Sound does not leave the zone: fighting and screams inside are not heard outside.
- Inside the zone everything is heard as usual.

### The way back

- Rank 3, ritual, a shift.
- An ally at Engaged range loses 2 psychosis and you gain 1.
- It does not touch psychosis 18: that needs a save or an ally, not a spell.

### Elemental Magic

#### Water Breathing

- Rank 1, Distance: self, Duration: one period
- Ensures possibility to breathe water as if it were air; saves from drowning effects
- Each additional power level expands effect to another creature or prolongs duration

#### Fire

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Using fire element, hero causes one item (but not living creature) to ignite
- If item is in character's hands - they must immediately drop it or take 1 damage
- If target is clothing or armor - target caught fire; flame intensity equals twice spell's final power (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4 for flame intensity)

#### Raise Earth

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Can break any non-magical object. Each point of power reduces quality of item by 1 point or deals 10 points of damage.

#### Suffocation

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: one round per power level
- Manipulating wind, hero literally pulls air from victim's lungs causing suffocation
- At start of each following round target takes 1 damage. Effects last number of rounds equal to spell power.
- Armor does not protect from this damage.
- If suffocating character becomes taken out - dies after d6 rounds (if not helped with healing check)
- While spell active, target cannot breathe or cry

#### Fireball

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Awakens fire in victim's heart, causing them to fall into rage and frenzy.
- Requires check against doubled potency (in modern variant: power 1 corresponds to two dice rank D, power 2 - rank C, etc.) vs. target's Insight.
- On success - target immediately attacks everyone within near distance and doesn't stop until they become taken out. Enemies fleeing from fight are excepted.
- Does not affect monsters and creatures without Wits score.

#### Drought

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Calling upon water element, hero sucks liquids from target's body. Target immediately takes damage equal to spell power (reduces Dexterity if using reduced characteristics).
- Armor does not affect this damage.

#### Stone Burst

- Rank 2, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Using earth element, hero can throw stones and debris at opponents. Attack damage equal to spell power.
- Armor works normally.

#### Flight

- Rank 2, Distance: self, Duration: one round per power level
- Hero can create small whirlwind that lifts them up. Allows moving two zones instead of one during action.
- Each additional power level either extends flight by one round or takes another creature with you.

#### Fireball

- Rank 3, Distance: far, Duration: instant
- Hero throws fireball at opponent, which explodes on contact, burning large area around.
- Primary target takes damage equal to spell power.
- Additionally, all within near distance from it (same zone) take fire attack with intensity equal to twice spell power.
- In modern variant: power 1 corresponds to intensity rank D, power 2 - rank C, power 3 - rank B, power 4+ - rank A

#### Whirlwind

- Rank 3, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Hero commands air to rotate, forming powerful whirlwind. Victims take damage equal to spell power (as if beaten with blunt weapon). Damage can be distributed among multiple victims in same zone.
- Additionally, all in whirlwind zone must make Body check to stay on feet.

## Blood Magic

### Hot Blood

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: one period
- Under influence of these spells, victim's blood boils, forcing strongest feelings such as passion, fear, or rage to surge forth. Target must in some way succumb to these emotions - how exactly depends on character and situation. Details determined by gamemaster. These spells cannot be woven into battle, and they don't affect monsters.

### Unburnable

- Rank 1, Distance: self, Duration: one shift
- Magician becomes immune to heat and cold (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4). Also takes no damage from fire.

### Kindle

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Magician can so intensely heat victim's blood that unfortunate bursts into flames. Damage equal to spell's final power. Additionally, victim is engulfed in flame (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4) with intensity equal to twice spell's final power.
- In modern variant: power 1 ensures intensity rank D, power 2 - rank C, power 3 - rank B, power 4+ - rank A.
- Armor does not protect from these spells.

### Blood Sacrifice

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Your hero can pump blood and energy contained within it from one creature to another, if both belong to same kind. Allows transferring certain amount of health points or resolve points (or any characteristic if damage tracked via reduction) equal to spell's final power. However this won't raise recipient's parameters above initial values. Reduced parameters restore as normal. Victim can resist: must make successful Insight check with penalty equal to spell's final power.

### Soul Binding

- Rank 3, Ritual, Distance: up close, Duration: variable
- This ritual allows extracting victim's soul from blood and binding it inside another object (e.g., mirror, precious stone or weapon). Target must make Insight check with penalty equal to spell's final power.
- On failure - victim becomes bound inside item for one shift.
- Binding for a day requires power 2; permanent (or duration you desire) - power 3.
- Gamemaster can assign condition for prisoner's release.
- While soul is bound inside item, body lies unconscious.
- Chance body killed or taken over by some restless spirit.

### Blood Curse

- Rank 3, Ritual, Distance: unlimited per power level per shift, Duration: one shift per power level
- Victim of these spells may become any living human-like creature whose name and approximate location gamemaster knows. Health or resolve (or any characteristic if damage tracked via reduction) decreases by 1 point per shift, until damage equal to spell's final power is dealt. Natural recovery does not work during curse effects.

### Blood Strength

- Rank 3, Distance: self, Duration: one round
- Using these spells, the magician accumulates energy in their own blood. The next spell in this round gains extra casting dice equal to the spell's final power. But very briefly: if the next casting does not happen in this round, the energy fades.

## Death Magic

### Infection

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Necromancer can create terrible infection that will torment enemies. Target must be human-like living creature. Victim contracts disease with toxicity equal to triple spell's final power (classic variant). In modern variant: power 1 ensures toxicity rank D, power 2 - rank C, etc.

### Mortal Gaze

- Rank 1, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Dark appearance of hero inspires uncertainty and fear in victim, reducing their resolve (or Empathy if using reduced characteristics). Damage equal to spell power. These spells only affect living human-like creatures.

### Winter Death

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: one round per power level
- Merciless mortal cold penetrates to victim's heart. Target suffers from hypothermia (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4). Result: health and resolve (Body and Wits if reduced characteristics) decrease by 1 point. This repeats each round until spells deal total damage equal to final power.
- These spells do not affect monsters.

### Raise Dead

- Rank 2, Ritual, Distance: up close, Duration: one shift per power level
- Main purpose of death magic - raising dead. In simplest form - ritual raises one dead animal or human-like creature as undead of first rank.
- Increasing power by 1 allows raising one more unit or increasing rank (affects all undead).
- Example: ritual power 2 can raise two first-rank undead; power 3 - two second-rank undead and etc.

### Talking to Dead

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: one shift per power level
- Your hero can converse with dead. Must be within up close distance from place of death or burial. Also must know name. Conversation lasts several minutes; can ask several simple questions. Gamemaster decides what dead can and will want to answer: dead are not always talkative! If remains relatively well-preserved, necromant can speak directly with body; otherwise hears disembodied voice of deceased in own head.
- This spell does not affect undead.

### Hand of Death

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Using these spells, necromant can reach victim's heart and squeeze life out. Damage equal to spell's final power. These spells only affect living human-like creatures.
- One extra casting die (not counted toward final power) can increase the distance to medium.

### Life Drain

- Rank 3, Ritual, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Your character can drain life forces from surrounding plants and animals. The next spell gains extra casting dice equal to the spell's final power. Ritual can only be performed in area covered with vegetation, so inside buildings and on rocky slopes of mountains it's useless. Upon ritual completion all living reasonable creatures and plants within up close distance - in same zone as necromancer - die.

### Horror

- Rank 3, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Necromant can instill strongest, unbearable fear: victim's reason fills with visions of aging and death. Target's resolve (or Wits and Empathy if reduced characteristics) decreases by amount equal to spell's final power. These spells only affect living human-like creatures.

## Symbolism Tradition

Magic uses runes, esoteric signs and symbols that reflect fundamental principles of world. This magic gains power that breaks forth when symbol impact bypasses, violates or bends various laws of microcosm.

### Sign Advantages

- **Perforation or resection**: Symbols drawn, carved or simply cut in air with magician's hand always increase final power of spell by 1 unit
- For writing symbol needs several minutes - cannot do in battle
- To carve symbol in stone requires at least one shift

### Symbol Activation

- Magician decides when exactly symbol should be activated
- Even carved, cut or drawn symbol activates only once

### Specific Symbol Spells

#### Fear

- Rank 1, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Symbol awakens deep fears in victim, reducing their resolve (or Wits) by spell's final power. These spells do not affect monsters.

#### Misdirection

- Rank 1, Distance: medium, Duration: instant
- Symbol lures victim: must make Insight check with penalty equal to spell's final power. On failure target must run, spending all ordinary actions until reaching symbol. Spells fade when victim reaches symbol or it moves for any reason.

#### Illusion

- Rank 2, Duration: one shift per power level
- Magician forces victim to see or hear things that aren't there; also can hide things that are actually visible.
- For small object needs first power level; for human-sized object - second; for house-sized object - third.
- Target's only victim of spell.
- Victim can recognize illusion successfully making Insight check with penalty equal to spell's potency
- These spells do not affect monsters.

#### Mind Manipulation

- Rank 2, Word of Power, Duration: one round
- Forces gamemaster character to abandon action they were about to take. This action must be insignificant: something that can be forgotten absentmindedly. Example: guard might let hero pass without asking who they are. Or someone might leave keys on table. Cannot be used in battle.

#### Blinding

- Rank 2, Duration: one shift per power level
- Symbol blinds victim if they fail Insight check with penalty equal to spell's potency. Consequences same as being in complete darkness (see "Other Dangers" Chapter 4).
- If target has no Wits score - automatically blinded.
- These spells do not affect monsters.

#### Gates

- Rank 3, Ritual, Distance: up close, Duration: one shift per power level
- This mighty ritual creates path from one world to another, tearing veil between them. Cannot be called safe; magician risks encountering various demons and other evil creatures on other side. Might use them for own purposes, or they might just devour him. In this situation binding demon (see Blood Magic) might come in handy.

#### Rune of Power

- Rank 3, Ritual, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Your hero charges a symbol with magical power. The needed symbol must be inscribed on an item, which then stores charges; the amount equals the spell's final power. The symbol can later be used when casting any spell, spending stored charges: each charge is one extra casting die. These spells don't require enchanting. Other magicians versed in rune craft can use your hero's runes if they manage to reach them, so keep them like pupil of eye!

## Magic Risk

### Random Side Effects

If at least one curse (1) is rolled during spell creation, roll d12 and consult side effects table:

| d12 Result | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| 1 | Due to magic next d6 days hero cannot sleep |
| 2 | Spell drains strength from hero. Takes 1 stress point |
| 3 | Spell injures body. Takes 1 damage point |
| 4 | Spell invokes magical disease with toxicity 2d6. During next shift hero and all within near distance risk infection |
| 5 | Spell affects not only target but also hero's ally or other unintended victim. Healing and helping spells also affect one opponent |
| 6 | Spell permanently changes hero's appearance. Gamemaster decides how |
| 7 | Spell blinds hero. Next several days hero acts as if constantly in complete darkness |
| 8 | Spell terrifies hero's mind. Make check per mental injury table |
| 9 | Spell breaks hero's bones. Make check per physical injury table |
| 10 | Magic attracts demon from other dimension. Demon appears within next shift and becomes source of numerous diverse problems |
| 11 | Spell strikes caster instead of target. Attacking spells hit caster; protective/healing spells deal damage |
| 12 | Magic tears dimension open; demon drags caster to other side - create new character; old hero returns as gamemaster character through d66 days but will be... different |

### Safe Magic (Reiteration)

If creating spell below rank of your magical specialization, can (but don't have to) roll one die less per point of difference. This reduces side effect chance - and enhancement chance. If reduced to 0 or fewer dice - no check needed: spell works exactly as intended.

## Spell Delivery Methods

### Magical Items

- Skilled magicians and druids can enchant inanimate objects to create magical traps or powerful artifacts
- Spell creation follows normal rules; extra casting dice bind the spell to the item
- Additional dice spent on binding do not count toward final power, but affect the enhancement check
- One extra casting die: the charm holds until activation, but not more than a day
- 2 additional casting dice: can hold indefinitely, but after activation still disperse
- 5 additional casting dice: spell permanently bound to item; can activate once per day, and each activation is a normal casting with its own dice and psychosis
- Activation method chosen by magician: speak certain phrase, open item, break it or throw on ground
- After activation spell works exactly as if created normally

### Power transfer

- Rank 3, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- The spell transfers power to another or takes it from the world around
- Casting is normal and costs nothing: the amount transferred is limited by the spell's final power
- If the target resists, the process is harder: the final power must exceed them
- Transferred power is extra casting dice for the receiver's next spell; they fade at the end of the scene

### Shapechange

- Druidic tradition power. Hero takes form of animal
- However many druids have completely mastered this art; tradition has many spells related to animals and their features

#### Cat's Step

- Rank 1, Distance: self, Duration: instant
- Druid mentally invokes cat spirit allowing silent movement. Can replace Stealth check. Provides automatic successes equal to final power.

#### Talk to Animals

- Rank 1, Distance: near, Duration: one period
- Charms allow communicating with mammals. Hero can ask number of questions equal to final power. Animal can tell what it saw, heard or sensed, but perception differs from human; answers may be hard to interpret. However animals never lie.

#### Eagle Sight

- Rank 1, Distance: far, Duration: one shift per power level
- Druid merges consciousness with hawk; obtains supernaturally sharp vision. Hero can survey landscape from horizon to horizon and as clearly examine any objects within far distance.
- Sees all details and recognizes facial features.

#### Bear's Strength

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Hero can strike enemy with bear's strength. Attack automatically succeeds and deals damage equal to spell power. Cannot be dodged, but normal armor protects.

#### Animal Mastery

- Rank 2, Distance: up close, Duration: one shift per power level
- Druid can subject both domestic and wild animals to their will. Animal can interrupt attack, track enemy, run needed distance, deliver message, allow hero to ride it or even attack opponents.
- Cannot control animal whose Body exceeds spell power by more than 2x.
- If animal is frightened, panicking or fighting, required power increases by 1 level.
- To make animal do something outside normal behavior (e.g., perform tricks) also requires increasing power by 1.

#### Wolf's Howl

- Rank 2, Distance: self, Duration: instant per power level
- Hero temporarily gains deer-like speed. These spells last one action (quick) and allow running at animal speed (see "Horseback Riding" Chapter 4). Additionally, hero receives Dexterity modifier equal to spell's final power.
- Casting these spells doesn't count as action. Must make enhancement and side effects checks before movement starts.

#### Bear Transformation

- Rank 3, Distance: self, Duration: instant
- Druid can completely change body, taking animal form. Animal's Body must not exceed twice final power.
- In animal form hero obtains its Body and Dexterity. In this form loses ability to speak and most of intellect: Smarts and Empathy decrease to 1 (but not below 1). Together with animal form hero receives its natural attacks.
- To revert to original form - must cast spell again.

#### First Path

- Rank 3, Distance: far, Duration: one shift per power level
- Hero can awaken primal feelings in hearts of surrounding people. Spell power must be not less than current target's Wits value. Example: hero can release boarish lethargy, feline laziness or sparrowish cowardice. How exactly victim reacts determined by gamemaster.
- If affecting entire crowd: for small crowd needs power level 2, for large crowd - 3, for entire settlement - 4.

#### Healing

- True calling of druids - helping nature and healing its wounds. Druids often turn to healing tradition, hence beloved by adventure seekers.

##### Laying on Hands

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Allows healing injured by laying on hands. Druid immediately restores target number of health points (Body or Dexterity if reduced characteristics) equal to spell's final power. Cannot heal self.

##### Spirit Healing

- Rank 1, Duration: instant
- Allows restoring target number of resolve points (Wits or Empathy if reduced characteristics) equal to spell's final power. Cannot heal self.

##### Restoration

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Heals target or another character from disease or neutralizes poison effect. Healing power must be equal or higher than third of disease toxicity or poison strength (rounded down).
- In modern variant: for disease/poison rank D needs power 1, rank C needs power 2, rank B needs power 3, rank A needs power 4.

##### Cleansing

- Rank 1, Distance: up close, Duration: instant
- Allows focusing nature's strength and bringing light to lost souls. Druid immediately restores target number of resolve points equal to spell's final power. Cannot heal self.
