# Root & Claw — psychosis

Magic has no resource: no points, no charges. A spell spends nothing but the caster. The price of magic is **psychosis**: it rises from curses on the dice and from shocks, falls on its own when the hero does not cast, and at its maximum hands the body to the beast.

Aura has nothing to do with it: aura is the world's opinion of the hero, a number from −5 to +5, and it is not spent on casting or on psi-powers.

## The psychosis track

| Psychosis | State | What it means at the table |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Clear | Magic has left no trace yet: the hero sleeps, eats and talks like a person. |
| 3-4 | Beast under the skin | Only your own people see the signs: pupils, smell, growling in sleep. No rule changes yet. |
| 5-6 | On the edge | The hero catches himself in beastly urges instead of words; to strangers he is already strange. |
| 7-8 | Breakdown close | Held together by habit and by his own. Being left alone is a bad idea. |
| 9 | One step from the beast | Any new curse or shock takes him to 10. |
| 10 | Instinct | An Empathy save at the start of each round; a failure shrinks the pool by one die. Pool gone means full transformation. |

Curses raise psychosis: +1 at rank 1, +2 at rank 2, +3 at rank 3 and above; a curse on a psi-power gives +1. Shocks give +1 to +3. Rest, meditation, your own people and sleep bring it down — see below. At 10 neither food nor talk works: only the save, or an ally.

## Casting costs nothing

Magic has no resource: no points, no charges. A spell spends nothing but the caster.

The price of magic is **psychosis**. It rises from curses on the dice and from shocks, falls on its own while the hero is not casting, and at the top hands the body to the beast.

Aura has nothing to do with it: aura is the world's opinion of a hero, a number from −5 to +5, and it is never spent on a casting or a psi-power.

## Casting dice

- The number of dice equals the **spell's rank**: rank 1 is one die, rank 2 is two, rank 3 is three, and so on.
- Main power equals the rank. Every success (6) adds 1 to the final power.
- Spell creation cannot fail.
- Special effort cannot be used when casting.
- **Safe magic.** Casting a spell below your specialization's rank, you may drop one die per point of difference. With no dice left the spell works exactly as intended: no successes, no curses, no psychosis.
- **Risky magic.** A spell one rank above your specialization always causes a side effect, but it still rolls as many dice as its rank.

## Psychosis

A counter from **0 to 10**. It is the only price of magic.

### What adds psychosis

| Event | Psychosis |
| --- | --- |
| A curse (1) on a rank 1 spell's die | +1 |
| A curse on a rank 2 spell's die (slow, expensive) | +2 |
| A curse on a rank 3 spell's die or higher | +3 |
| A curse on a psi-power's die | +1 |
| The death of a friend, or of someone the hero protected | +3 |
| A comrade's betrayal | +2 |
| A broken oath, especially a tusk oath | +2 |
| Losing a limb or a mutation | +2 |
| Watching the Garden take someone you knew | +2 |
| A killing that was not needed | +1 |
| Public humiliation while carrying your aura | +1 |
| A night without fire or a dry room | +1 |

A curse does not cancel the spell: it may work and still leave a mark. The d12 side effect is rolled as usual — see `05-magic.md`.

### What removes psychosis

- **Passively:** −1 at the end of a round in which the hero did not cast or use a psi-power.
- **Meditation:** a full shift of quiet removes **3** — a third of the maximum of 10.
- **Company:** a scene of talk or a shared meal with allies, once per scene, removes 1. Real care — someone stays with the hero rather than merely nearby — removes 2.
- **Sleep:** a night in a dry room by a fire removes 1 more.
- Neither food nor talk works at psychosis 10: that needs a save or an ally.

## Psychosis 10: instincts

At the top, reason gives way to the beast. This is **not berserk**: the hero does not attack everyone, they behave as their animal does, and they do it with purpose.

| Lineage | What the hero does |
| --- | --- |
| Raccoon-kin | Takes apart, opens and carries off anything shiny; cannot pass a lock |
| Rodent-kin | Digs a burrow, wedges into a gap, hoards supplies |
| Hare-kin | Runs and does not stop until out of breath; will not be held |
| Mustelid-kin | Plays, wrestles, will not let go of whoever is nearby |
| Bird-kin | Climbs as high as possible and calls; will not come down |
| Reptile-kin | Freezes, waits, basks; does not move until it is safe |
| Cat-kin | Stalks and strikes from cover at whatever moves |
| Dog-kin | Guards the "pack" — the nearest allies — and will not let them leave |
| Boar-kin | Goes straight ahead and breaks what stands in the way |

The GM describes the behaviour, the player runs the hero inside it. Acting against instinct needs a save, or a direct order from whoever the hero accepts as leader.

## The save against instinct: Empathy

The save is rolled with **Empathy** — it asks whether the hero can still hear other people.

- At the start of every round at psychosis 10, the hero rolls the save.
- **The save pool** is Empathy dice: as many d6 as the hero's Empathy in the classic variant, the Empathy rank die in the modern one.
- **Success** (6+): psychosis drops to 9 and the hero acts normally.
- **Failure:** the save pool loses one die for this episode and the hero stays in instinct. The longer it lasts, the harder the way back.
- **Pool empty:** full transformation. The hero becomes a beast run by the GM — for good, short of a ritual nobody in the Overgrowth has performed twice.
- **An ally** can bring the hero back without the hero's roll: a slow action at Engaged and a Persuasion or Animal Ken check. Success ends the instinct and drops psychosis to 9. Failure costs the ally a round and, at the GM's discretion, a wound.
