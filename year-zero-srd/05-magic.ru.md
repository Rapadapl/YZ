# Year Zero Engine - Магия (Русский)

## Магические традиции

### 7 традиций магии

#### 3 друидских Tradition
1. **Очищение** - Healing, protection, nature connection
2. **Преобразование** - Shapechange, elemental manipulation
3. **Дивинация** - Foreglimpse, revelation, tracking

#### 4 сорcerерских Tradition
1. **Кровь** - Blood magic, life force manipulation
2. **Смерть** - Death magic, necromancy, spirit communication
3. **Символизм** - Symbolic magic, rune work, sigils
4. **Элементализм** - Elemental magic, fire/ice/lightning control

## Обучение магии

### How to learn magic
- Requires a teacher who already knows the tradition
- Both mentor and student must spend a full shift on training
- Teacher must make a successful Insight check at the end of the shift
- Cost: 10 experience points per tradition rank increase

### Magic rank system
- Ranks A to D, similar to characteristics and skills
- Initial rank is D for all traditions
- 3 steps to raise ranks during character creation
- Can lower one tradition from C to D to gain an additional step

## Создание заклинаний

### Spell creation process
1. Determine the effect you want
2. Choose the tradition that best fits
3. Define the risk and enhancement
4. Establish potential side effects
5. Gamemaster approval required

### Risk and enhancement
- **Risk**: Each spell carries inherent risk - consult the risk table
- **Enhancement**: Can increase spell power at additional cost
- Balance between risk and power is key to effective spellcasting

### Common spells
- List of typical spells per tradition (examples provided in original document)
- Can be modified or combined for unique effects

## Риск и побочные эффекты

### Magic risk table
| Risk Level | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| Minor | Temporary condition, minor inconvenience |
| Moderate | More significant effect, potential complication |
| Major | Severe consequence, possible permanent effect |
| Catastrophic | Story-altering event, character transformation |

### Common side effects
- Fatigue and exhaustion
- Mental stress or confusion
- Physical drain or damage
- Attraction of unwanted attention
- Unintended magical side effects

## Ритуалы

### Ritual magic
- More powerful than standard spells
- Requires special components and setup
- Takes longer to cast (shift or more)
- Greater risk and potential for enhancement

### Ritual components
- Magical artifacts
- Specific locations or times
- Sacrifices or offerings
- Complex procedures

## Безопасные правила магии

### Safe magic rules
- Certain traditions have safer approaches
- Can reduce risk at cost of power
- Gamemaster discretion applies
- Always have contingency plans
