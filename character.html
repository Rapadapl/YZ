At the table

What comes up every round.

Your turn

  • One slow action and one fast action, or two fast actions.
  • Fast: move a zone, aim, seek cover, draw, get up, retreat, block.
  • Slow: brawl, shoot, reload, first aid, persuade, crawl, a psi-power.
  • Free: drop prone, say a few words — on your own turn only.

Initiative

  • Cards 1–10, drawn once when the fight starts.
  • Lowest number acts first; actions refresh at the start of each round.
  • One card can cover a whole group of identical beasts.

Ranges

  • Engaged — right next to you.
  • Short — a few metres, in your own zone.
  • Medium — up to 25 m, the next zone.
  • Long — about 100 m, four zones.
  • Extreme — up to a kilometre.
  • Dim light: −2 to Observation and to ranged attacks.

Movement

  • A fast action takes you to a neighbouring zone; no roll.
  • Cluttered zone: Acrobatics, or you fall.
  • Crawling is a slow action; in a cramped zone it is the only movement.
  • Leaving Engaged range takes Acrobatics, or the enemy gets a free attack.

Healing

  • First aid on the broken: Healing, a slow action, each success removes 1 damage or 1 stress.
  • Otherwise 1 damage and 1 stress recover per shift, if fed, watered and warm.
  • Lethal crit: a Stamina death save at the time limit; Healing of the same length raises the limit one step.
  • A critical injury heals in days; a carer's daily Healing roll makes the day count double.

Damage and armour

  • Damage is the weapon value plus 1 for each extra success.
  • Armour and cover subtract from it; a broken character takes critical injuries.
  • Pushing costs 1 damage (Strength, Agility) or 1 stress (Wits, Empathy) per bane.
  • Pushing a roll can never kill you.

Mutations

  • Bio-mutations are always on and cost no aura.
  • A psi-power is a slow action and costs nothing; a curse adds 1 psychosis.
  • Roll Wits plus grade: +1/+2/+3 dice, or d6/d8/d10.
  • Extra successes buy damage, targets or duration.

Aura

  • Aura runs from −5 to +5 and starts at 0; it is never spent.
  • Light: +1 die to Animal Ken and Persuasion with beasts and tribes.
  • Dark: +1 die to Brawl and Shooting against a foe who has seen you draw blood; −1 to Animal Ken.
  • Mercy raises it; killing the defenceless lowers it.
